About Us

Founded to make higher education rigorous, not exclusive.

Kestrel College opened in 1968 with three departments and a hundred students. Today it holds eighteen departments across the arts, sciences, and commerce — still run on the same principle: teach in small rooms, mark honestly, and keep tuition within reach.

Governance

How the college is run

Leadership

Office of the Principal

Oversees academic policy, admissions standards, and the college's affiliation with the State University.

Faculty

Departmental Councils

Each department sets its own syllabus additions and lab requirements within university guidelines.

Students

Student Council

Elected representatives from every year sit on the grievance, cultural, and library committees.

Mission

What we're trying to do

We measure the college by two things: whether graduates can defend an argument in writing, and whether they leave able to teach themselves something the syllabus never covered. Everything else — the labs, the library hours, the faculty we hire — is built to serve those two outcomes.
